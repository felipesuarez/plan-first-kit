# Tidewater Pottery Studio: Product requirements

## Goal

Visitors and members can book classes, join the studio, and see when their pottery is ready to collect, without messaging the studio on Instagram or phoning the front desk.

## Who uses it

- **Visitors**: people looking at classes for the first time.
- **Members**: about 400 people with a monthly studio membership.
- **Instructors**: 6 part-time teachers who each run several classes a week.
- **Studio staff**: the 3 people who run the front desk and the kilns.

## Features

| Feature | Who uses it | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Class listing and booking | Visitors and members | A booked seat appears on the instructor's class list and a confirmation email arrives within a minute |
| Membership sign-up and monthly billing | Visitor becomes member | The new member is charged, sees "Active" on their account, and receives a receipt |
| My pieces tracker | Members read, staff update | A member sees each piece move from Drying to Ready to collect, and gets an email when it is ready |
| Class waitlist | Visitors and members | When a seat opens, the first person on the list is offered it and has 12 hours to accept |
| Studio dashboard | Staff and instructors | Today's classes, seats left and pieces ready to collect are all on one screen |

## Not in this version

An online shop for clay and tools, a phone app, gift vouchers, live-streamed classes, and kids' birthday-party bookings.
